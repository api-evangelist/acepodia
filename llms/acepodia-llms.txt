## About

Acepodia was founded in 2017 by Dr. Patrick Y. Yang and Dr. Sonny Hsiao. Its core Antibody-Cell
Conjugation (ACC) technology derives from the bioorthogonal click chemistry developed in Professor
Carolyn Bertozzi's laboratory, work recognised with the 2022 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. ACC links
tumor-targeting antibodies directly to allogeneic immune effector cells — gamma delta 2 T cells and
natural killer cells — without genetic engineering, producing what the company calls antibody cell
effector (ACE) therapies. A second platform, AD2C, performs site-specific conjugation of multiple
linker-payloads to an antibody.

## Pipeline

- ACE1831 — anti-CD20 armed allogeneic gamma delta 2 T cell therapy; Phase 1 in non-Hodgkin lymphoma,
  with a program in IgG4-related disease.
- ACE2016 — anti-EGFR allogeneic gamma delta 2 T cell therapy for EGFR-expressing solid tumors;
  IND cleared by the FDA, Phase 1.
- AD2C-Oncology — GPC3-targeting program in hepatocellular carcinoma; preclinical.
